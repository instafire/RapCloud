FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Q

What is RapR?

RapR is a free underground rap platform where artists can upload, share, and promote their rap music videos. It features daily charts, trending tags, artist analytics, and a community-driven For You feed — built specifically for the underground scene.

Q

Is RapR free to use?

Yes, RapR is completely free for artists and fans. Create an account, upload your rap videos, and start building your audience with no hidden fees or paid tiers.

Q

How do I upload my rap video to RapR?

Upload a video file directly from your device, paste a direct video URL to import, or record a new video using your camera. Add your song title, producer credits, lyrics, and hashtags before publishing. You can also save unfinished drops as drafts.

Q

How do underground rappers get discovered on RapR?

Rappers get discovered through daily, weekly, and monthly charts ranked by real engagement, trending genre tags, the personalized For You feed, and organic community tools like shoutouts, collab invites, and mentions. No paid promotion needed.
